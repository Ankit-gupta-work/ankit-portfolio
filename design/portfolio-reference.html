How I think

Three decisions from JobPing.

Every piece of infrastructure has to earn its place. Here's why these did.

Why RabbitMQ?

The problem

Database polling wakes up every N minutes whether or not there's new data.

The decision

RabbitMQ wakes the worker only when a message arrives.

− while(true) { poll(); sleep(N); }
+ on message → process

Why Redis for 4 things?

The problem

Caching, a distributed lock, rate limiting and a JWT blacklist — four separate needs.

The decision

One infrastructure component, four real problems.

cachelockrate limiterjwt blacklist

Why delta updates for preferences?

The problem

Delete-all and re-insert breaks audit trails and creates race conditions.

The decision

Track the delta. Apply the diff.

+ added: [RabbitMQ]
− removed: [jQuery]
= unchanged: untouched
